Pulse Cube

Space, ↑ or tap to jump. Hold to keep jumping. Ship: hold to fly up. Wave: hold to zig up, let go to zag down. Ball: tap to flip gravity. Swing: tap to flip gravity in mid-air. Spider: tap to teleport to the other side. UFO: tap to flap upward. Robot: hold longer to jump higher. Watch out for spinning sawblades. Gravity portals: yellow flips you upside down, blue flips you back. Mini portal shrinks you, Big grows you back. Speed portals change how fast you go. Dual portal (orange) splits you in two: a mirror twin on the ceiling copies every move. Both must survive. The blue Single portal joins you back. Grab the 3 gold coins hidden in each level. Orbs: tap or hold inside one. Yellow jumps, pink jumps less, blue flips gravity.
